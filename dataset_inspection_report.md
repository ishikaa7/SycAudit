- `sycophancy-false-premises/*_labeled.jsonl`, `*_completions.jsonl` and `*_labels.json` are paired: prompts referenced by `prompt_id`/`fact_id` connect completions to labels; several `.json` files are aggregate analysis results (entropy/kdg/kappa), and `prompt_pressure_labels.json` maps full prompt text to NEUTRAL/LEADIN/SYCOPHANTIC-style labels.
